1. Regulatory Architecture & Tariff Mechanics

Understanding how fee exemptions, caps, and tiered interchanges scale across purchase values and payment instruments.

The NPCI fee structure guarantees zero MDR for direct bank-to-bank transfers under ₹2,000. However, transactions exceeding ₹2,000 incur an ad valorem charge of 0.40% capped at ₹300. Additionally, Prepaid Payment Instruments (PPI Wallets) attract up to 1.10% interchange, while RuPay Credit Cards on UPI incur standard credit MDRs of 1.50% to 2.50%.

MDR(T) = 0 if T ≤ 2000 else min(0.004 × T, 300)

Consumer Fall-Out of Informal P2P Evasion

When merchants bypass commercial gateways to dodge reclassification, payments are registered as private personal transfers. Consumers lose access to automated chargeback workflows, formal payment tracing, institutional dispute resolution, and digital tax invoice validity for warranty claims.

5. Ecosystem Monetization & Point-of-Sale Friction

How core payment restrictions drive peripheral platform fees and counter friction.

Figure 3: Platform monetization pivot from cashbacks to convenience surcharges.

Artificial Bill-Splitting & Velocity Blocks

For bills over ₹2,000, merchants frequently split single transactions into multiple ₹1,500 transfers. This triggers automated bank security flags, leading to payment halts, delayed checkout queues, and cluttered bank statements.

Peripheral Platform Convenience Fees

Fintech applications levy non-negotiable convenience surcharges of ₹1 to ₹3 on mobile recharges and utility bill payments through BBPS to monetize zero-MDR users.

6. Policy Assurances vs. Operational Realities

Direct side-by-side comparison of official institutional stances versus actual market behavior.

Policy Stance

"Consumers will never be charged for UPI payments."

Section 10A of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act mandates zero surcharges directly on transaction screens.

Market Reality

MDR expenses are incorporated into business operating costs, driving generalized price increases across product inventories.

Policy Stance

"Small local vendors will remain zero-MDR."

The P2PM exemption framework shields sellers with monthly receipts up to ₹1,00,000.

Market Reality

Urban Kiranas handling ₹5,000 daily quickly exceed limits, forcing them to push cash payments or use personal accounts.

Policy Stance

"PPI Wallet interchange does not impact consumers."

Interchange fees up to 1.10% are strictly charged to merchants accepting wallet payments.

Market Reality

Merchants decline wallet transactions at checkout, while platforms levy top-up fees on wallet loads.

Policy Stance

"Zero MDR protects everyday micro-purchases."

Exempting transactions up to ₹2,000 ensures essential daily commerce remains unburdened.

Market Reality

The ₹2,000 threshold creates artificial checkout friction, forcing bill-splitting and causing transaction declines.
